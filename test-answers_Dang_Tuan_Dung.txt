Q45 [2.4] (4 marks): Describe how symmetric encryption works and give its main problem. [4]
Answer: Symmetric encryption uses the same secret key to encrypt and decrypt the data. Both the sender and the receiver must have it. However, the problem with symmetric encryption is that the secret key has to be sent to the receiver first. If it's intercepted, anyone can decrypt every message.
